Blue Board

Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Blue Board helps contractors run jobs, schedule crew, track expenses and see job profit. This page explains what information the app keeps and why.

If you don't create an account

Everything you enter (jobs, expenses, photos, crew and schedule) stays on your device. We never receive it.

If you create an account

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, so it can sync between your devices and your crew. We keep:

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored encrypted), to sign you in.
  • Your company's data: jobs, prices, expenses, estimates, invoices, payments, schedule, crew names, pay rates, payroll, punch lists, time off and your logo.
  • Your customers' details that you enter: names, phone numbers, email and street addresses, and their job history.
  • Photos you add to jobs, receipts and daily reports.
  • Daily reports, task check-offs and time-off requests sent by crew members.
  • A notification token for each phone that allows notifications, so we can send schedule and job updates to it. It's removed when you sign out.
  • Clock-in and clock-out records for crew members, including the time and the phone's location at that moment. Location is read only when someone taps Clock in or Clock out, never in the background.

Who can see it

  • The company owner can see all of the company's data.
  • Crew members can see the schedule, job names and addresses, and their own hours and pay. They can't see prices, expenses, profit or anyone else's pay.
  • Your customers see only the estimates, invoices and customer pages you send them (see below).
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Estimates and invoices you send

When you send an estimate or invoice, Blue Board creates a private link with a long random code. Anyone who has the link can view that one document, so share it only with your customer. The page shows your business details, the customer's name and address, and the line items. A customer who approves an estimate types their name, and we record it with the date and time. If you delete the document in Blue Board, the link stops working.

Customer pages you send

A business can send a customer a page for their job with key dates and choices to make, such as a tile color. It works the same way: a private link with a long random code that anyone with the link can open. The business also gets a short customer code the customer can type into the Blue Board app, and anyone with that code can open the same page. The page shows the business's name and contact details, the job name and address, the customer's first name, and the dates, descriptions, prices, photos, files and voice memos the business added. Those photos, files and voice memos can be opened by anyone who has their exact link. When the customer makes a choice, we record what they picked, any note they typed, the name they enter and the time, and we notify the business. Removing an item deletes its files.

Card payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Each business connects its own Stripe account, and customers pay on Stripe's secure checkout page. Card numbers go directly to Stripe; Blue Board never sees or stores them. We keep only the amount, the date and which invoice was paid. Stripe's handling of payment information is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

Phone notifications

If you allow notifications, the iPhone app sends Apple a request for a notification token. We send notifications through Apple's push service with a short message, for example that you've been scheduled on a job. You can turn notifications off any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Camera, photos and location

The app asks for the camera and photo library only when you add a photo, for the microphone only when you record a voice memo, and for location only when you clock in or out. You can turn these off in your phone's Settings.

When you scan a receipt in the iPhone app, the text is read on your phone by Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo isn't sent anywhere to be read. If you're signed in, the receipt photo is saved to your account like any other photo.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account (crew members: My account → Delete account). This permanently deletes your login and, for a company owner, all of the company's data, photos, customers, shared document links and customer pages. Payment records held by Stripe stay in your Stripe account. Data saved only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear the browser's site data.

Children

Blue Board is a business tool and isn't meant for children under 13.
