Common questions

How do I add my crew?
Create an account, open Crew, pick a person and tap Invite. Send them the message; they open the link, create an account and they're on your crew.

A crew member can't clock in.
Make sure location is turned on for Blue Board in the phone's Settings, and that they're scheduled on a job.

How do I send an estimate or invoice?
Open the job, tap + Estimate or + Invoice, add the lines and save. Then tap Send to customer to text or email them a link. Customers can approve estimates from that link.

How do customers pay by card?
Go to Settings → Card payments and connect your Stripe account. After that, every invoice link shows a Pay by card button, and payments are added to the invoice automatically.

How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings → Backup and export a backup file. With an account, your data also syncs to every device you sign in on.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete account (crew members: My account → Delete account). See the privacy policy for what gets deleted.
