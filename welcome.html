Questions

Do I need a credit card for the free trial?

No. Create an account and use Blue Board for 14 days with your whole crew. You don't enter a card to start.

Does my crew have to pay too?

No. Your plan covers your crew. They download the app or open it in a browser, join with your invite link, and clock in from their own phones.

Can you move my data from Jobber or CompanyCam?

Yes, for free. You can import CSV files and photos yourself in Settings, or email support@getblueboard.com and we'll do it for you.

What happens if my crew grows?

Switch to the next plan whenever you need more crew members. Your jobs and history stay the same.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no contract. Cancel in Settings → Plan & billing on the website. You can export a full backup of your jobs and records whenever you like.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Anyone on Android, or on a computer, uses Blue Board in their web browser and can add it to their home screen like an app. iPhones can use the app or the browser.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Photos, daily reports and checked tasks save on the phone and upload as soon as there's signal again.

Is my data safe?

Your company's data is stored securely and only people you invite can see it. Read the privacy policy for details.
